Neighbors Helping Neighbors

A Hand Up, Not a Hand Out

The St. Vincent de Paul Society conference at St. Luke visits families privately in their homes, where neighbors are free to discuss their struggles and needs. Our Vincentians listen, offer humble advice, and render assistance. We also coordinate annual parish drives and support sister conferences beyond our own.
